# MORI BAR GRAN

## Basic Info
- **Address:** 13F, Sakura Marks Ginza 612, 6-12-12 Ginza, Chuo City, Tokyo 104-0061
- **Price:** $$$ (up to $$$$$)
- **Rating:** 4.5 (up to 5)
- **Tags:** cocktail
- **Google Maps:** [View Map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/pRD8MyrFVKH94W8M9?g_st=ipc)  
- **URL:** https://mori.bar/

## Highlights
- Master Mori's signature martini.
- Enjoy a drink on the rooftop terrace, with views of both Tokyo Tower and Tokyo Skytree.
- Opens at noon, making it perfect for a daytime drink.
- Seats are relatively easy to find compared with other Ginza bars.

## Notes
- Master Mori is usually here from 8–10 p.m.; outside those hours, he is typically at Mori Bar. [Link](./moribar.md)
